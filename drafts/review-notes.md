## Hosting wording

identifies the Kazakhstan operator, and explains that connected services can
process information in other countries. It does not assert that all data
stays in Germany, that a Kazakhstan copy exists, or that users can waive a
storage requirement by accepting the policy.

This is the most supportable wording with the facts available. It is not a
legal determination about the deployment. No infrastructure change was made.
