instructs us how to process the information. We generally act as its processor
for those activities. The business's privacy notice and our applicable
data-processing agreement address that relationship. Roles depend on the
actual processing, not simply the label used in a contract.

If you contact another business through an Evercom-powered widget or channel,
please contact that business first about its use of your information. You
can also contact us, and we will help identify the appropriate route.

## 9. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and our role, you may have rights to obtain access
to your personal information, correct it, request erasure, restrict or object
to processing, and receive certain information in a portable form. Some rights
have conditions or exceptions; they are not identical in every country.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness
of processing before withdrawal. **Where applicable, you can object to direct
marketing at any time and to processing based on legitimate interests on
grounds relating to your situation.**

Send a request to **support@evercom.app**. Explain your connection to Evercom
and what you need. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify
your identity or authority. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details.
We will handle requests within the period required by applicable law and
explain any permitted refusal or extension.

For information controlled by a business using Evercom, that business normally
decides how to respond. We will assist it in accordance with our obligations
and will not treat a customer's instruction as authority to disregard rights
that the law gives you against us directly.

You may complain to the competent data-protection authority without first
complaining to us. Where the GDPR applies, this can include the authority in
the country of your habitual residence or workplace, or where the alleged
infringement occurred. For UK matters, you may contact the
[Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
