# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy explains how POSTMYPOST LLC handles personal information when you visit our website, request a demo or use Evercom, and when businesses use Evercom to communicate with their customers.

For account administration, demo enquiries and our own business operations, we decide why and how personal information is used. Under laws that use these terms, we act as a controller for those activities.

When a business uses Evercom to manage its customers' messages and records, that business generally determines the purposes and instructs us how to process the information. If you contact another business through an Evercom-powered widget or connected channel, contact that business first about its use of your information. You can also contact us for help identifying the appropriate route. When you chat directly with Evercom through our own widget, we handle that conversation for our own support or sales purposes.

## 2. Information we handle

**Account and team information:** names, email addresses, account credentials, workspace membership, permissions and settings provided by you or a workspace administrator.

**Customer conversations and records:** messages, comments, replies, attachments, contact information, channel identifiers, conversation history, tickets and CRM information supplied through a widget, connected platform, import or API.

**Knowledge and automation content:** documents, help content, AI instructions, workflow configuration, and AI inputs and outputs. These can contain personal information depending on what you or your customers provide.

**Connected-account information:** account or page identifiers, profile information, permissions and credentials such as access tokens needed to operate a connection you authorise.

**Demo requests and correspondence:** your name, email, requested appointment and timezone, booking status, optional company and discussion details, and information you provide when contacting us.

**Technical information:** IP addresses and request information, browser or device information, session identifiers, and operational or security records generated through use of the service. The information involved depends on the feature and configuration.

**Transaction information:** purchase, subscription, receipt and refund information needed to administer your access and respond to billing enquiries. Lemon Squeezy handles checkout and payment information under its own privacy policy. Do not send full payment-card details to our support team or through chat.

Information comes from you, your workspace administrator, people communicating with your business, authorised integrations and the operation of the service. Fields required to handle a request are identified when collected. For demo requests, name, email and appointment information are necessary; company and discussion details are optional.

## 3. Why we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide customer messaging and related records, operate integrations, run the AI, knowledge and workflow features you use, answer enquiries, arrange demos, administer subscriptions and refunds, investigate faults or abuse, protect the service and meet legal obligations.

For customer content processed on behalf of a business, our processing follows its service instructions and our agreement, subject to applicable law. Those instructions do not by themselves authorise unrelated advertising or training a general-purpose AI model.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to our own processing, we rely on the following grounds as appropriate to the activity: performing a contract with you or taking steps you request before a contract; legitimate interests in administering a business relationship, supporting its staff and protecting a reliable service, where those interests are not overridden by your rights; and compliance with legal obligations, such as required record keeping or binding legal demands. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we must obtain it. Accepting a privacy policy is not blanket consent to every use of personal information.

## 4. AI and conversation information

When an AI feature is used, relevant message text, conversation history, instructions and knowledge excerpts may be sent to the configured AI provider to perform the task and return an answer. These inputs can contain personal information, such as a name, email address, delivery address or order details that someone includes in their message.

This applies both to businesses using Evercom for their customers and to AI-assisted conversations with Evercom itself. Messages and AI replies can remain in the conversation history. External AI processing is separate from storing that conversation in Evercom.

Evercom supports AI processing through OpenAI-compatible services, including OpenAI. The provider used depends on the AI configuration. For information about the provider and processing arrangements relevant to your workspace, contact support@evercom.app. OpenAI describes its own processing in its [business data privacy information](https://openai.com/business-data/) for business customers. API content is governed by the applicable customer agreements.

Using an AI model to generate an answer is distinct from training a model. This policy does not grant a general permission to use customer content for unrelated model training. Provider handling, including retention, depends on the service and applicable arrangements; keeping a conversation in Evercom does not mean a provider keeps an identical copy or follows the same retention period.

AI output may be inaccurate. A business using Evercom decides how it deploys replies and workflows and is responsible for any notices and safeguards required for its use of automated processing.

If a business transfer involves personal information, applicable privacy obligations and any required notices continue to apply.

## 7. Retention, account closure and deletion

We retain information according to its purpose, our service obligations, customer instructions and applicable legal requirements. Account and workspace content is retained to provide the service and maintain the conversation and records your business uses. Closing an account or workspace restricts access but does not automatically erase all underlying records.

You can request deletion or ask about retrieving your information at **support@evercom.app**. We assess requests according to our role and applicable law, including whether information belongs to an active business workspace. We will explain the process and relevant limitations. This policy does not promise immediate or automatic erasure on closure.

Any retention after closure must have a continuing purpose, such as completing a deletion request, resolving an outstanding transaction or dispute, security investigation or legally required record keeping. Retained information must not be used for unrelated purposes. The period depends on that purpose, any applicable statutory period and when the matter is resolved; information should be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed.

Booking records are removed from the booking service after their scheduled end time during its next successful daily cleanup. This does not automatically delete the separate messages in Telegram or our email correspondence. Those communications are retained for handling enquiries and follow-up or resolving related issues, subject to applicable retention and deletion requirements.

Disconnecting a channel does not by itself erase conversation history or copies held by the external platform. Backup copies, where maintained, are separate from active records and may remain until overwritten or expired under the backup cycle. Information subject to legal retention requirements or an applicable preservation obligation may need to be retained for that limited purpose. Contact us about the arrangements applicable to your information.

## 8. Browser storage and security

The application and widget use browser storage and session identifiers to support functions such as authentication and continuity of a conversation. Removing stored information or restricting browser storage can sign you out or affect whether a previous widget conversation is recognised. External services, such as payment checkout, may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their policies.

We do not treat use of the website as permission for non-essential tracking. Where applicable law requires consent for such tracking, it must be obtained separately.

We use access controls and other technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access and misuse. No online service can promise absolute security. Protect your credentials, restrict workspace access appropriately and tell us promptly if you suspect a security problem. Security-incident notifications are handled according to applicable legal and contractual obligations.

## 9. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law and our role, you may have rights to access or correct your information, request erasure, restrict or object to processing, and receive certain information in a portable form. These rights have conditions and exceptions and are not identical in every country.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. **Where applicable, you can object to direct marketing at any time and to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your situation.**

Send requests to **support@evercom.app**, explaining your connection to Evercom and what you need. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify identity or authority. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details. We will handle requests within the period required by applicable law and explain any permitted refusal or extension.

For information controlled by a business using Evercom, that business normally decides how to respond. We assist it in accordance with our obligations; this does not remove rights that applicable law gives you against us directly.

You may complain to the competent data-protection authority without first complaining to us. Where the GDPR applies, this can include the authority in the country of your habitual residence or workplace, or where an alleged infringement occurred. For UK matters, you may contact the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
